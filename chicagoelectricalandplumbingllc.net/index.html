Reliable and

Chicago Electrical & Plumbing LLC is a licensed and insured general contracting company serving Chicago and the surrounding suburbs.

We specialize in electrical work, plumbing services, handyman services, remodeling, and full property renovations for residential and commercial clients.

Our handyman division provides fast and reliable solutions for small and large repairs, including fixture replacements, drywall patching, painting, door and trim installation, cabinet repairs, flooring, light carpentry, and general maintenance.
