Company

Commercial capacity,
local accountability.

SJP is a full-service commercial and municipal plumbing and site-utilities contractor serving Northwest Indiana and Chicagoland. The portfolio spans schools and universities, medical buildings, multi-unit dwellings, hotels, design-build plumbing, and underground site utilities — alongside complete residential plumbing service.

Work ranges from large commercial builds to smaller construction efforts, including LEED projects and Capital Development Board jobs. SJP is a member of the U.S. Green Building Council and operates under three guiding principles: a respectful workplace environment, embracing diversity, and maintaining the highest industry standards.
